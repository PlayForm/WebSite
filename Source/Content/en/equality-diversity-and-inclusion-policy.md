# EQUALITY, DIVERSITY AND INCLUSION POLICY

Effective date: 11.23.2026 / November 23rd 2026\
Last updated: 11.23.2026 / November 23rd 2026

This policy states how PlayForm ltd. (the **Company**) treats the people who work
for it, apply to it, contract with it and use what it builds. It is the umbrella
document of the Company's equality framework: the [Gender Equality Plan] sets out
the concrete measures, targets and reporting that make this commitment
verifiable, and each of the supporting policies in the
[Documentation](/Documentation) register expands one part of it.

## Purpose

The Company builds software and operates cloud services. That work is done by
people, for people, and the quality of the result depends on who is allowed to
contribute to it. This policy exists to make sure that access to the Company, and
the way it treats the people inside it, is decided by ability and conduct, never
by sex, gender, background or any other characteristic unrelated to the work.

It also exists because equality commitments that are not written down, resourced
and measured are not commitments. Every obligation in this policy names the person
responsible for it and the document in which its progress is reported.

## Policy Statement

The Company is committed to:

- **Equal treatment** - every person is treated with dignity and respect, in
  every interaction, at every level.
- **Equal opportunity** - recruitment, pay, promotion, training, assignment and
  exit decisions are made on documented, job-related criteria.
- **Equal representation** - decision-making bodies reflect the people the
  Company serves, and the Company acts when they do not.
- **A working environment free of discrimination, harassment and violence**,
  including gender-based violence and sexual harassment.
- **Accountability** - each commitment above is owned, measured and published.

Top management of the Company adopts this policy and is accountable for it. It is
published on the Company's website and applies from the effective date above.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- all personnel, whatever their contract type - employment, fixed term,
  part-time, interim, agency, internship or volunteer;
- candidates at every stage of recruitment;
- contractors, consultants and suppliers, and their personnel while working for
  the Company;
- collaborators, research partners and consortium members;
- the community that contributes to or uses the Company's open work.

It covers every place the Company's work happens: offices, remote work, travel,
events, conferences, online meetings, chat channels, repositories and any other
space where the Company's people act in a professional capacity.

## Protected Characteristics

The Company does not tolerate less favourable treatment on the basis of:

- sex, including pregnancy, maternity, paternity and breastfeeding;
- gender identity and gender expression;
- sexual orientation and sex characteristics;
- age;
- disability, chronic illness and neurodivergence;
- race, ethnicity, nationality and national or social origin;
- religion or belief;
- family, marital or parental status, and care responsibilities;
- socio-economic background, education and level of experience;
- body size and physical appearance;
- part-time, fixed-term, remote or agency working status;
- any other ground protected by the law applicable to the person concerned.

The list is not closed. A characteristic that is not named here is not therefore
permitted as a reason for unequal treatment.

## Legal and Policy Framework

The Company operates from Bulgaria and works with partners across the European
Union and beyond. This policy is written to satisfy the strictest obligation that
applies to a given activity, and it draws on:

- the Treaty on European Union and the Treaty on the Functioning of the European
  Union, including the principle of equal pay for equal work and work of equal
  value;
- the Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union;
- Directive 2006/54/EC on equal opportunities and equal treatment of men and women
  in matters of employment and occupation;
- Directive 2000/78/EC establishing a general framework for equal treatment in
  employment and occupation;
- Directive (EU) 2023/970 on pay transparency and enforcement mechanisms;
- the Bulgarian Labour Code and the Bulgarian Law on Protection against
  Discrimination;
- the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as implemented in the
  Company's [Personal Data Protection Policy].

Where a national law, a funding agreement or a partner's policy sets a higher
standard than this policy, that higher standard applies.

## Governance and Responsibilities

**Top management (Managing Director)** adopts this policy, appoints the Gender
Equality Officer, approves the budget for the measures in the
[Gender Equality Plan], and signs the monitoring reports.

**Gender Equality Officer (GEO)** is the named owner of the equality framework.
The role is resourced with dedicated time and a budget line. The GEO coordinates
data collection, maintains the register of measures, prepares the biennial
monitoring report, advises managers, and is one of the confidential routes for
reporting a concern.

**Gender Equality Committee (GEC)** is a cross-functional body that meets at least
quarterly. It reviews progress against the targets in the
[Gender Equality Plan], reviews anonymised case statistics, and proposes
corrective action. It includes the GEO, a member of top management, and
representatives of the personnel.

**Managers** apply this policy in every decision they take about people, complete
the training required by the [Gender Equality Training and Awareness Programme],
and escalate concerns immediately.

**All personnel** treat colleagues, candidates, partners and users with respect,
complete the annual awareness module, and report conduct that conflicts with this
policy.

## Equal Opportunity in Practice

The policy is only as strong as the decisions it governs. The Company therefore
applies it at the following decision points, each governed by a supporting policy:

- **Hiring** - gender-neutral job design and advertising, structured interviews,
  documented scoring, panels that are not homogeneous. See the
  [Recruitment and Career Progression Policy].
- **Pay** - equal pay for equal work and work of equal value, annual pay-gap
  analysis by grade and by role family, no questions about salary history. See the
  [Recruitment and Career Progression Policy].
- **Promotion and assignment** - published criteria, annual calibration across
  managers, and a rule that statutory leave does not count against time in role.
- **Leadership** - balanced shortlists, balanced panels, sponsorship, and
  published representation targets. See the
  [Gender Balance in Leadership and Decision-Making Policy].
- **Working conditions** - predictable scheduling, flexible and remote work,
  leave for care, and a culture that does not reward presence over output. See the
  [Work-Life Balance and Organisational Culture Policy].
- **What the Company builds** - research, product design, data and documentation
  are reviewed for gender relevance and bias. See the
  [Gender Dimension in Research and Teaching Policy].
- **Safety** - prevention of, reporting of, and response to gender-based violence
  and sexual harassment. See the
  [Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy].

## Reasonable Accommodation

The Company provides reasonable accommodation for disability, chronic illness,
neurodivergence, pregnancy, religious observance and care responsibilities. This
includes adjustments to equipment, schedule, workspace, communication and
workload. Requests are made to a manager or to the GEO, are answered within ten
working days, and are reviewed with the person concerned. An adjustment is refused
only where it imposes a disproportionate burden, and a refusal is given in writing
with reasons.

## Zero Tolerance of Discrimination, Harassment and Victimisation

The Company has zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, sexual harassment,
bullying, stalking, and any form of gender-based violence, whether it occurs in
person, online, at a Company event, on a business trip or in a private channel
used for work. Zero tolerance means that a substantiated case results in action up
to and including termination of the relationship, and, where the conduct may be
criminal, in a report to the competent authorities.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern, supports a report or
participates in an investigation is itself a disciplinary matter, treated as
seriously as the original conduct.

## Reporting a Concern

A concern may be raised through any of the following routes. A person is never
required to raise a concern with the person the concern is about, or with that
person's manager.

- **Directly** - to a manager, to the GEO, or to any member of the Gender
  Equality Committee.
- **In writing** - to Support@PlayForm.Cloud, marked for the attention of the
  Gender Equality Officer. The message is routed to the GEO and to top management
  only.
- **Anonymously** - through the confidential reporting channel described in the
  [Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy].
- **Externally** - to the Bulgarian Commission for Protection against
  Discrimination, the labour inspectorate, or the competent authorities.

Every report is acknowledged within two working days, handled confidentially, and
investigated under the procedure in the
[Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy]. The person reporting is
informed of the outcome and of the support available to them.

## Positive Action and Targets

Equal treatment alone does not remove a disadvantage that already exists. The
Company therefore takes positive action - measures that are proportionate, aimed at
a defined under-representation, and time-limited. These measures never override
merit: they widen the pool from which a merit decision is made.

The current targets, the measures that serve them, and the date by which each is
due are set out in the [Gender Equality Plan]. Progress is reported in the
biennial monitoring report described in the
[Gender Equality Data and Monitoring Framework].

## Monitoring and Reporting

The Company collects sex and gender disaggregated data on personnel, recruitment,
pay, promotion, training, leave and exits, and reports on it at least every two
years. The definitions, the indicators and the publication cycle are fixed in the
[Gender Equality Data and Monitoring Framework]; the reports themselves are
published in the [Documentation](/Documentation) register.

Data is collected for the purpose of monitoring equality, is minimised, is
protected as described in the [Personal Data Protection Policy], and is never used
to take a decision about an individual.

## Training

Every person covered by this policy completes the awareness training defined in the
[Gender Equality Training and Awareness Programme]: an annual module for all
personnel, a longer module for managers and decision-makers, and a module for
members of recruitment panels before they sit on one. Training covers unconscious
bias, inclusive language, the reporting routes and the standards expected in this
policy.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the Gender Equality Committee and approved
again by top management. It is also reviewed whenever the Company's structure, its
workforce or its legal obligations change, and after every biennial monitoring
report. Previous versions remain available on request.

# GENDER EQUALITY DATA AND MONITORING FRAMEWORK

Effective date: 11.23.2026 / November 23rd 2026\
Last updated: 11.23.2026 / November 23rd 2026

This framework carries the measurement part of the [Gender Equality Plan]: the
collection of sex and gender disaggregated data on personnel, and reporting on it at
least every two years. It fixes what is collected, how each figure is computed, how
often it is published, and what protects the people the data is about.

## Purpose

A commitment to equality that is not measured cannot be shown to be kept. This
framework makes the Company's equality data reproducible: a reader of a monitoring
report can see how a number was produced, what population it covers, and what was
excluded. It also sets the limits - the data exists to monitor the Company as an
organisation, never to judge an individual.

## Legal basis and principles

The data is processed under the [Personal Data Protection Policy], on the legal bases
that apply to monitoring equal treatment and to the Company's obligations as an
employer, including the safeguards for special categories of data. Five principles
govern it:

- **Purpose limitation.** The data is used for monitoring equality, for the biennial
  report, and for defending or responding to a legal claim. It is not used to make a
  decision about an individual.
- **Minimisation.** Only the fields the indicators require are collected. No data is
  collected "in case it is useful".
- **Voluntary self-declaration.** Sex and gender are self-declared. "Prefer not to
  say" is always available, and a refusal is never recorded as a problem.
- **Transparency.** The categories, the indicators and the publication cycle are
  published here, and every report states how its figures were produced.
- **Protection.** Small numbers are suppressed, access is restricted, and the data is
  kept separately from performance records.

## Data categories

- **Headcount** - sex or gender as self-declared, grade, role family, contract type,
  working pattern (full-time, part-time, remote), start date and, where applicable,
  end date.
- **Recruitment** - applicants, shortlisted candidates and appointments by stage, and
  the sex of the person appointed; panel composition.
- **Pay** - base pay and total pay by grade and role family, on an annualised
  full-time-equivalent basis.
- **Promotion** - promotions by grade and sex, and the sex of the people eligible at
  each grade.
- **Training and development** - hours and spend per person by sex, and completion of
  the mandatory modules.
- **Leave** - parental, paternity, adoption, carer's and sick leave taken by sex, and
  the return-to-role outcome.
- **Exits** - voluntary exits by sex, grade and reason category, including exits within
  twelve months of returning from parental leave.
- **Flexible working** - take-up of part-time, remote and compressed arrangements by sex
  and grade.
- **Cases** - reports of gender-based violence and sexual harassment, recorded in
  anonymised, aggregated form only: route, category, time to acknowledgement and to
  conclusion, and outcome.
- **Research and product reviews** - the count of projects and releases with a completed
  gender-relevance check or bias review.

## Collection and self-declaration

- Personnel declare their sex or gender in the personnel system, with a "prefer not to
  say" option and the ability to change the answer at any time.
- New joiners are asked during onboarding; existing personnel are asked once at the
  start of the first collection cycle, and are told what the data is for and who can
  see it.
- Where a person has not declared, they are counted in the total but excluded from the
  disaggregated analysis, and the share of undeclared answers is reported so that the
  reader can judge the coverage.
- The Company does not infer sex or gender from a name, a photograph or any other
  proxy.
- Pay, promotion, training and leave data are taken from the Company's own systems
  rather than from a separate survey, so that the figures reconcile with the payroll.

## Quality, correction and access

- The dataset is checked each cycle for completeness, duplicates and inconsistencies
  between systems; the check and its result are recorded.
- A person may ask what is held about them and have it corrected; the request is
  answered within one month.
- The aggregated dataset, without identifying fields, is available to the Gender
  Equality Committee and to the Gender Equality Officer. Individual records are
  available only to the People and Culture function and, where necessary, to top
  management.
- The data protection contact named in the [Personal Data Protection Policy] is
  consulted before any new field is added.

## Indicators and their computation

- **Headcount composition** - count and share of personnel by sex within each grade,
  contract type and working pattern, at the last day of the reference period.
- **Leadership composition** - for each decision-making body, the share of seats held
  by each sex, at the last day of the reference period.
- **Applicant funnel** - for each stage (application, shortlist, appointment), the count
  and share by sex, and the stage-to-stage conversion rate by sex.
- **Promotion rate** - the number of promotions in the period divided by the headcount
  at the start of the period, computed by sex and grade.
- **Promotion parity ratio** - the promotion rate of the under-represented sex divided
  by the promotion rate of the other sex. A value of 1.0 is parity; below 1.0 means the
  under-represented sex is promoted less often.
- **Gender pay gap** - the difference between the mean pay of women and the mean pay of
  men, expressed as a percentage of the mean pay of men, and the same computation using
  the median. Computed separately on base pay and on total pay, and by grade and role
  family, so that the composition of the workforce is not mistaken for unequal pay for
  the same work.
- **Training participation** - training hours and spend per person by sex, and the
  completion rate of each mandatory module by audience.
- **Leave and return** - days of each leave type taken per person by sex, and the share
  of people returning from parental leave who remain in the same or an equivalent role
  twelve months later.
- **Voluntary exit rate** - voluntary exits divided by average headcount, by sex and
  grade, and the share of exits within twelve months of a return from parental leave.
- **Flexible working take-up** - the share of personnel on each arrangement, by sex and
  grade.
- **Case statistics** - the count of reports by route and category, the median time to
  acknowledgement and to conclusion, and the outcomes, in anonymised aggregate only.
- **Review coverage** - the share of research projects and product cycles with a
  completed gender-relevance check, and the share of releases with a documented bias
  review.

## Analysis methods

- Every indicator is reported against the baseline of the preceding report, and against
  the target in the [Gender Equality Plan], with an explicit statement when a target was
  missed and why.
- Pay gaps are analysed by grade and role family before any organisation-wide figure is
  reported, because an organisation-wide figure that moves with the composition of the
  workforce is not evidence of unequal pay for equal work.
- Where a figure rests on a small population, it is reported as a count rather than as a
  percentage, or suppressed under the rule below.
- Findings are reviewed by the Gender Equality Committee, which records the
  interpretation and the corrective action in its minutes. The interpretation is
  published with the report, so that a number is never left to speak for itself.

## Reporting cycle and publication

- Data is collected continuously and consolidated for a reference year ending
  31 December.
- The Gender Equality Officer prepares the **Gender Equality Monitoring Report at least
  every two years**. The first report covers the 2026 reference year and is published by
  **30 June 2027**. Subsequent reports are published in 2029, 2031 and so on, within six
  months of the end of the reporting period.
- The report is published in the [Documentation](/Documentation) register, at a stable
  address, and previous reports remain available so that progress can be traced.
- The report contains: the indicator values with the baseline; progress against each
  target; the pay-gap analysis; the anonymised case statistics; training completion; the
  coverage of the research and product reviews; and the corrective actions decided.
- An annual internal review is prepared for top management between publications, and its
  conclusions are recorded in the Committee's minutes.

## Small-number suppression

Any published cell that contains fewer than five people is suppressed, and the
suppression is marked in the table rather than left as a gap. Where suppression would
make a figure derivable by subtraction, the adjacent cell is suppressed as well. The
rule applies to every published table without exception, including the case statistics.

## Retention and access control

- Personnel equality data is retained for the duration of the employment relationship
  and for the period required for the defence of legal claims afterwards, and is then
  deleted or anonymised.
- Case files are retained separately, under the
  [Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy].
- Published reports contain aggregated data only, and are retained permanently as part of
  the Company's public record.
- Access to the underlying dataset is role-based, logged, and reviewed annually.

## Roles and responsibilities

**Gender Equality Officer** - owns the framework, prepares the dataset and the report,
and presents the findings to the Committee.

**People and Culture function** - maintains the source data, runs the collection, and
answers correction requests.

**Gender Equality Committee** - reviews the data, records the interpretation, and
decides the corrective actions.

**Top management** - approves the report before publication and signs it.

**Data protection contact** - advises on the legal basis, on new fields, and on any
request that touches individual rights.

Documentation

Everything PlayForm publishes, in one tree. Each document is versioned, dated, owned by a named role and reviewed on a stated cycle. This page is the index for all of them: it is generated from the documents themselves, so a document that is published is listed here, and a document that is listed here exists.

The register has two branches. Company and Legal holds the terms the Company offers and the obligations it accepts towards the people it serves and the data it holds. Equality, Diversity and Inclusion holds the Gender Equality Plan and the policies that carry each of its measures, targets and evidence.

Coverage

Nothing here is listed by hand. The tree is built from the documents themselves, so a published document cannot be missing from the index, and a listed document cannot be absent.

Adoption

Versioned, owned, reviewed

Every document carries a version, a date, the role that approved it, the role that owns it and the cycle on which it is reviewed. The version published at its address is the version in force.

The signed original stays with the Company. It is provided to partners, customers, auditors and funders on request, together with the records that show the commitments being kept.

Equality, Diversity and Inclusion

9

The Company's equality framework: the Gender Equality Plan and the policies that carry each of its measures, targets and evidence.

  1. Equality, Diversity and Inclusion Policy Umbrella policy

    The Company's commitment to equal treatment, dignity at work and equal opportunity for everyone it employs, contracts or serves.

  2. Gender Equality Plan Equality plan

    The Company's plan for gender equality: what it commits to, who owns each commitment, how it is measured and how it is reported.

  3. Work-Life Balance and Organisational Culture Policy Working conditions

    Flexible working, leave for care, predictable scheduling and the cultural rules that stop presence being mistaken for performance.

  4. Gender Balance in Leadership and Decision-Making Policy Leadership

    Representation targets for every body that decides, balanced shortlists and panels, sponsorship, and transparency in appointments.

  5. Recruitment and Career Progression Policy Hiring and pay

    Structured, criteria-based hiring, equal pay and pay transparency, and promotion rules that do not penalise leave or part-time work.

  6. Gender Dimension in Research and Teaching Policy Research and product

    How sex and gender enter research questions, datasets, models, product design and documentation - and how each is checked before release.

  7. Gender-Based Violence and Sexual Harassment Policy Conduct and safety

    Zero tolerance, the reporting routes, the investigation procedure with fixed timelines, support for the person affected, and sanctions.

  8. Gender Equality Data and Monitoring Framework Measurement

    What the Company measures, how it collects it, how each indicator is computed, and how the biennial monitoring report is published.

  9. Gender Equality Training and Awareness Programme Learning

    The curriculum, audiences, schedule and evidence for gender equality training - including unconscious bias for staff and decision-makers.

How to read a document

Each document opens with a control block: its version, its date, the role that approved it, the role that owns it and its review cycle. The version published at its address is the version in force; the signed original is retained by the Company and provided to funders, validators and auditors on request. Where a document refers to another, the reference is a link into this tree, so the chain from a commitment to the evidence for it can be followed in both directions.
